Who we are

A Sri Lankan workroom built around one job: dressing your team properly.

SAM & Co. is a uniform and promotional-apparel manufacturer based in Sri Lanka. We work directly with corporates, hotels, restaurants, schools and industrial sites to design, cut and sew uniforms that hold up to a full shift and still look considered at the end of it.

Fabric, fit and finishing are handled in-house, so an order placed as a sketch or a reference photo comes back as a wearable sample — not a guess. From there we scale to whatever your roster needs, restocking as your team grows.

Five steps, start to finish.
